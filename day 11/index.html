Display:Flex (Parents Property) Justify Content

The Justify Content:Aligns the flexible container's items when the items do not use all avilable space on the main-axis(horizontel).

The justify content property can also used on a grid container to align grid items in the inline direction.for pages in english, inline direction is left to right and block direction is downward.

"flex-start" in the context of CSS Flexbox refers to a value that determine the aligment of items within aflex containeralong the main axis, typically placing them at the start of the container.
